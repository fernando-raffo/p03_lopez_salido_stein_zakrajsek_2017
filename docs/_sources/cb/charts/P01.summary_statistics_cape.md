# Chart: Summary Statistics: CAPE vs. Subsequent 10-Year S&P Return (1929-present)
Scatter of Shiller CAPE (P/E10) at year t against the cumulative S&P total return realized over the following 10 years, with a fitted line showing the valuation/mean-reversion relationship.

[Full Screen Chart](../download_chart/P01/summary_statistics_cape.html)




x-axis: `pe10`; y-axis: 10-year-forward sum of `sp_return` (the log total return implied by `sp500_price` and `dividend`), both from `shiller_data_annual.parquet`. Years within 10 years of the end of the sample are dropped for lack of a forward window.
