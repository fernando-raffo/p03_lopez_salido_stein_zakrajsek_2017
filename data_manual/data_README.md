# Manually-Created Data

This folder is used to hold manually created data that cannot be easily replicated.

## Files

- **`greenwood_hanson_hys_historical.csv`**: the annual high-yield share
  (fraction of nonfinancial corporate bond issuance rated below investment
  grade), 1926-2008, transcribed from Table 2 of Greenwood and Hanson (2013),
  "Issuer Quality and Corporate Bond Returns," *Review of Financial Studies*
  26(6), 1483-1525. The pre-1983 years are spliced by the original authors
  from printed sources (Hickman 1960; Atkinson 1967; hand-collected Moody's
  Bond Surveys) that exist only in print, so this series cannot be pulled from
  any database.
